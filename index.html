A few helpful answers
Before you begin.
Is Field & Fence a real contractor?
No. This is a fictional website concept made to demonstrate clear service information and a guided project inquiry. The illustrations are concepts, not photographs of client work.
Does this form send an email or book a visit?
No. It validates the example information and shows a request preview. A client version would need a connected inbox or booking system and a tested delivery process before launch.
Can this be adapted to a different business?
Yes. The services, project questions, service area, branding, and illustrations can be replaced with the business's verified information and approved work samples.
Does the demo generate a project price?
No. A contractor would need to confirm the actual scope, materials, access, and measurements before quoting a price.
